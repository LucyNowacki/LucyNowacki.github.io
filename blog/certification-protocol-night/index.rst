This interactive companion follows a 2×2 spectral-certification toy example
from contour separation to the final operator gate and preservation of the
enclosed spectral count. Its thirteen steps connect the written argument to
the geometry of balls, ellipsoids, inverse bounds and uncertainty.

.. TEASER_END

Explore the argument
====================

Use the numbered navigation to move between steps. The **Geometry**,
**Argument** and **Connections** tabs explain the objects, the inequalities
and how the steps fit together. The diagrams adapt to the available width;
on a narrow screen, scroll down to see each panel.

Reading the pictures
====================

The contour lives in the spectral plane. The image and inverse-image diagrams
describe vector spaces, while the matrix-enclosure diagrams describe matrix
space. Their labels distinguish certified lower bounds, inverse upper bounds
and quantities evaluated at a selected point.

The moving pictures illustrate the supplied certification argument. A sampled
singular value or a rotated section does not itself certify a whole-contour
minimum. The final endpoint is an abstract toy operator.
