Gridder 2 is an educational dexterity game that brings a little arcade chaos
into exploring calculus. Guide Gridder around a coordinate grid, close loops
to capture areas, and reveal the mathematical features hidden in each level.
There are creatures to dodge, routes to plan and plenty of functions to
explore along the way.

`Play Gridder 2 </gridder-v2/>`_

.. TEASER_END

How to play
===========

Choose a function and a difficulty, then guide Gridder along the grid lines.
Close a loop and the enclosed area fills in. Capture the target rectangles to
reveal the point or graph requested by the level. You'll need to plan your
route and steer carefully while the creatures move around the board.

Each function brings its own family of monsters. There are also pools of water
with leeches and a shark, as well as other creatures and surprises as you play.
Gridder starts each function with seven lives; captured areas remain after a
catch. You can turn the creatures off if you would like to concentrate on the
mathematics.

Discover the mathematics
========================

Depending on the function, levels invite you to find minima, maxima and
inflection points, or reveal the first and second derivative, an inverse on a
stated branch, and a normal line. Each mission has a clue above the board, so
you can think about what you expect to find before tracing a route.

The mathematics panel explains the current case, including formulas, reasoning,
interpretation and the relevant domain or branch. Complete a level to reveal
its worked solution. You can float the panel to keep the explanation close while
you play.

Make it your own
================

Use the keyboard arrow keys or the on-screen direction buttons to move, and
press Escape to pause. The game has a full-screen button, movement settings,
optional target hints, and separate controls for music and sound effects.
Touch controls are available, and the mobile experience is still evolving.
