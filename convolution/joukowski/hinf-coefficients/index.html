Analyticity forces coefficient decay

The second-kind kernel decays geometrically on an ellipse. The contour formula transfers that decay to the Legendre coefficient, with an explicit cost for the function’s size and the boundary’s length.

\[|a_n(f)|\le(2n+1)C_\infty(\varrho)\,\|f\|_{H^\infty(E_\varrho)}\,\varrho^{-n}.\]

The proof in three steps

1 · Start strictly inside

Choose \(1<s<\varrho\). The function is continuous on the smaller closed ellipse, so the contour formula applies there. Its real-line coefficient is still the same \(a_n(f)\).

\[\|f\|_{A(E_s)}\le M_\varrho:=\|f\|_{H^\infty(E_\varrho)}.\]

2 · Add absolute contributions

The triangle inequality replaces the complex differential by arclength. Bound the function by \(M_\varrho\) and the kernel by its geometric tail.

\[|a_n(f)|\le\frac{2n+1}{2\pi}|\partial E_s|M_\varrho\sup_{\partial E_s}|Q_n|.\]
\[\sup_{\partial E_s}|Q_n|\le\frac{2s^{-n}}{s-s^{-1}}.\]

3 · Approach the outer radius

Let \(s\uparrow\varrho\). The perimeter and the explicit kernel bound depend continuously on the radius. A continuous trace of a general bounded holomorphic function on the outer boundary is not required.

\[C_\infty(\varrho)=\frac{|\partial E_\varrho|}{\pi(\varrho-\varrho^{-1})}.\]
Source, normalization and numerical meaning

This lesson follows Subsection 3.2.6 — \(H^\infty\) coefficient bounds from the second-kind kernel and Lemma 3.25, in thesis Section 3.2. Bandtlow’s original result is EDMD–Legendre Lemma 1.9.

The normalization is \(P_n(1)=1\) and \(a_n(f)=\frac{2n+1}{2}\int_{-1}^1 f(x)P_n(x)\,dx\). All ellipse radii exceed one.

The plotted function is analytic beyond every displayed closed ellipse. Its pole stays at \(2.5\), and its outer norm is the closed expression shown above. Its coefficients are evaluated using 80 positive Laurent terms at \(z=2.5+\sqrt{5.25}\), so they do not depend on the displayed ellipse radius.

The perimeter option uses 2,048-point angular quadrature. It illustrates the exact perimeter formula but is not an interval enclosure. The explicit fallback uses the analytic estimate

\[|\partial E_\varrho|\le\pi(\varrho+\varrho^{-1}),\qquad C_\infty(\varrho)\le\frac{\varrho+\varrho^{-1}}{\varrho-\varrho^{-1}}.\]

At the final slider position, the inner curve displays the limiting expression \(s\uparrow\varrho\). Between integer degrees, hover values interpolate the plotted curves; they are not coefficients of noninteger Legendre modes. All computed values use ordinary floating-point arithmetic.
