Apply the Legendre weight

0:00 Now recover a coefficient. A Legendre coefficient combines values from the whole real interval. For degree two, multiply x squared by P two of x and integrate from minus one to one.

0:12 Include the normalization. The classical Legendre polynomial equals one at the right endpoint. Its coefficient formula carries a factor of n plus one half. For degree two, that factor is five halves.

0:25 Keep the sign of the weight. P two is one half of the quantity three x squared minus one. Its zeros are plus and minus one over the square root of three. The product is negative between them except at zero, and positive toward both ends.

0:40 Add the signed areas. Integrating x squared times P two gives four fifteenths. Multiply by five halves and the coefficient is two thirds. This is the number we will now recover from the ellipse.
