The angular bound

Start with a sine wave. Multiply it by the Legendre value, and take k to be a positive integer.

Sigma is a signed integral. Integrate P sub n of cosine t times sine of k t, from zero to pi.

The Legendre factor has magnitude at most one. The product stays between the upper and lower absolute sine envelopes.

Positive and negative areas can cancel. The magnitude of the signed integral is at most the integral of the absolute product.

Look at k equal to three. The upper envelope has three lobes, each of width pi over three and area two thirds.

Count the lobes. For every positive integer k, the k lobes have area two over k each, totaling two.

Every coefficient has magnitude at most two. This holds for every Legendre degree and every positive integer frequency.
