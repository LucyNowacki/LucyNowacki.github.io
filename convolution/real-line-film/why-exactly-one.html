Why exactly 1?

Why exactly one? Can a Legendre mode exceed one in absolute value?

Every phase has modulus one. Each carries a positive weight, in every degree.

Normalization gives the total weight. At angle zero, all phases equal one. The weights sum to P sub n of one, which is one.

The disk is convex. It contains every weighted mean of these points, so the absolute value is at most one.

The bound covers the whole interval. Every x from minus one to one is cosine t for some angle.

Now line up the phases at angle zero. The mean reaches one at the right endpoint. The supremum is exactly one, in every degree.
