A weighted mean

Give each phase point a mass. Their balance point gives the height of the graph.

Degree two has three phases. Their angles are twice t, zero, and minus twice t.

The weights add to one. Three eighths, one quarter, and three eighths are all positive.

Set t to sixty degrees. The three phase points form a triangle. Their weighted mean is its balance point.

The two mirror points have equal mass. Their imaginary parts cancel, so the balance point sits on the real axis.

Read the balance point. At x equal to one half, P two takes the value minus one eighth.
